Matte faceted shapes, soft atmospheric distance, strong readable silhouettes, editorial storybook lighting, gentle paper grain, no black cartoon outlines. Leave quiet negative space for HTML title copy. No text, logo, weapons aimed at the viewer, photorealism, existing characters, brand marks, UI, spheres, capsules, or recognizable commercial-game designs.

Outputs: `wildkin-portraits-source.png` (971×1619 source) and `wildkin-portraits.jpg` (719×1200 optimized derivative).

> Create a tall original naturalist field-guide portrait plate containing five clearly separated low-poly creature studies on warm parchment. The subjects are Burramble, a busy low hexapod inside an open wicker-seed shell with tiny amber insets and fiber sprigs; Flintusk, a stubborn wedge-bodied quadruped with three slate plates and asymmetrical tuning-fork tusks; Coaloon, a shy balloon-bellied tripod with a glowing ceramic chimney crest and bellows-like feet; Wickerwing, a watchful broad woven glider with hooked feet and one long off-center vane; and Rippletail, a gentle flat kite-shaped amphibian with four small feet and two flowing ribbon tails. Give each creature a small, relevant work prop or environmental vignette—raised bed, cloudstone, forge ember, sunbark, or water/hauling bundle—while keeping every silhouette distinct. Use deep ink, parchment, leaf, sun gold, clay red, river teal, slate, and pale steel; matte faceted editorial illustration, tactile paper and tool marks, restrained shadows, no oversized mascot eyes. No labels, readable text, logo, grid copied from a game UI, reference characters, brand marks, balls, capsules, firearms, or artist imitation.

> Create an original square, edge-friendly UI surface texture for a tactile frontier field journal. Base it on quiet parchment cream with subtle handmade paper fibers, faint botanical impressions, sparse fired-clay flecks, restrained charcoal tool scratches, and occasional thin braided river-teal cord marks. Keep the center and most of the surface low contrast so dark ink text remains highly readable; move stronger marks toward loose corners and edges without creating a literal frame. Warm, matte, lightly weathered, optimistic, practical, no photorealistic tabletop. No readable writing, alphabet characters, numbers, logos, icons, maps, badges, recognizable game motifs, stains that resemble faces, heavy grunge, or third-party branding.
